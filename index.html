Offline: no internet, server, account, or installation is required.

KDP Book Builder: mix Easy, Medium, and Hard puzzles in one interior, add front matter and section dividers, and send all answer keys to the back automatically.

Expanded library: word, number, logic, Sudoku-variant, and math puzzle generators.

Quality tools: validation still runs behind the scenes for Quality Check and Preflight, but verification labels are no longer printed on puzzle pages.

Difficulty: Book difficulty is controlled by the Easy / Medium / Hard puzzle counts, or per-row difficulty in Mixed Puzzle Book. The old standalone difficulty selector has been removed.

Presets & editing: choose a book preset, use Large Print, open Book Overview, regenerate individual puzzles, delete them, or move them up/down without rebuilding the whole book.

Variety: High/Maximum variety changes pattern families, sequence rules, cage layouts, path styles, search directions, and cryptogram mappings so large books feel less repetitive.

KDP readiness: Preflight now checks puzzle/solution matching, mixed-book counts, page-count consistency, frames/safe-area heuristics, blank pages, section starts, and export readiness.

Mixed books: choose puzzle type, difficulty, and quantity for every mixed-book row. Design: frame templates, page typography, multi-puzzle layouts, mirrored page numbers, right-hand section starts, spread preview, and final preflight checks.

Validation: Sudoku, Kakuro, and Slitherlink are solver-checked. The generator rejects a puzzle unless it finds exactly one solution. Mazes are generated with a verified entrance-to-exit path.

Smart assembly: tiny sections avoid wasteful divider pages, larger section dividers stay right-page aligned, solution pages adapt their density to each puzzle type, and PDF/JSON exports receive organized filenames automatically.

How-to pages: Each puzzle type now gets a visual instruction page with mini grids, arrows, highlighted cells, and worked examples.
PDF: choose “Create PDF,” then select Save as PDF. For KDP interiors, keep browser scaling at 100% and margins set to none/default as appropriate.
